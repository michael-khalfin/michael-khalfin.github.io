## Background
To Simon, science of the 'artificial' is the science of 'design'; the sciences of the artificial are relevant to "all fields that create designs to perform tasks or fulfill goals and functions."

#### Traditional Model
- Adam Smith, Léon Walras - Homo economicus ("Economic man"): rational, self-interested individual who makes decision to maximize personal utility and wealth
- Challenged by Herbert Simon (Bounded rationality)
  - The "satisficing" human (as opposed to optimizing)
- Later *proven false* by Daniel Kahneman, Amos Tversky, and Richard Thaler (birth of Behavioral Economics)

#### Intelligence Architectures
- General Problem Solver (GPS): could in theory solve any problem
- See Wikipedia articles on ACT-R, Deliberative Agent, and Reactive Agent preceding Transformer.

### GOFAI
- Supposedly, the attempt to formalize AI as high level symbolic AI is what contributed to "so many AI winters" (Blaise Agüera y Arcas)

## Notes
- pgs 1-2 Natural science: make the wonderful commonplace
- pg 3 **Thesis**: Traditional science is great at explaining (1) natural law, ok at explaining (2) human purpose (e.g., social science), but there is no language for explaining the interface between (1), (2), i.e., artificial objects created to map the human goal onto the natural laws of the world. The book addresses this science of *design*.
**"Artifacts"** Markets, algorithms, companies; Physical objects, symbolic systems, social systems (including AI)
- pg 5 The "pristine empiricist" is David Hume, and the idea called "Is-Ought Problem"/"Hume's Guillotine". Goals (ought) are separate from facts (is). Science can't tell you what to want. Humans must decide the goals.

The designer's job is to take human goals ("oughts") and use the facts of the world ("is") to create an artifact that achieves the goal.

- pg 6 **Artifact** redefined: Interface between environments
  - "inner" environment, the substance and organization of the artifact itself
  - "outer" environment, the surroundings in which it operates
  - Bird, airplane: same inner&outer environments
